## Sources Used For Tribes Category

To improve accuracy for the new `tribes` category, questions were drafted from cross-referenced material on Gulf social history, tribal terminology, and diwaniya/customs culture.

Primary references reviewed:

1. [Dewaniya (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dewaniya)
  - Background on diwaniya/diwaniya function in Kuwait and Gulf social life.
2. [IASA - The Diwaniyya in Urban Kuwaiti Society](https://iasarabia.org/research/the-diwaniyya-in-urban-kuwaiti-society-a-reflection-of-socio-spatial-and-diplomatic-realities/)
  - Social/political role of diwaniya and its contemporary relevance.
3. [Princeton - Of Sand or Soil: Genealogy and Tribal Belonging in Saudi Arabia](http://assets.press.princeton.edu/chapters/i10630.pdf)
  - Genealogy and tribal belonging in Saudi social history.
4. [GlobalSecurity - Saudi Arabia Tribes](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/world/gulf/sa-tribes.htm)
  - Broad overview of major tribal structures and historical context.
5. [GlobalSecurity - Kuwait Tribalism](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/world/gulf/kuwait-tribalism.htm)
  - Context on hadhar/bedu social composition and tribal influence.
6. [EveryCulture - Bedouin Kinship](https://www.everyculture.com/Africa-Middle-East/Bedouin-Kinship.html)
  - Segmentary lineage terms and kinship structure (bayt/fakhadh/qabila level framing).

### Editorial rules applied

- Avoided contested lineage claims and "best/worst/richest tribe" style questions.
- Focused on high-confidence cultural/terminology items:
  - diwaniya functions
  - hospitality norms
  - migration drivers (water/pasture/trade)
  - lineage terminology (`fakhadh`, lineage vs custom)
- Kept distractors close in type and wording to the correct answer.
